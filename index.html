FAQ

How do I steer?
Touch the left or right side of the screen to steer. Touch both sides at the same time to fire your nitro when the bar is full.
How do I play with friends?
Tap MULTIPLAYER, create a room and share the 4-letter room code. Your friends enter the code to join. You can also join public rooms. An internet connection is needed.
How do I move my progress to a new phone?
Open SETTINGS and copy your Backup Code. On the new phone, choose "Restore your progress" and enter the code. Keep this code private.
How do I remove ad breaks?
Buy "Remove Ad Breaks" or Gold Membership with diamonds in the STORE. The optional "Watch and continue" ad after a crash is always your choice.
How do I delete my data?
Use "Reset Progress" in SETTINGS, or email us and we'll delete your saved data.
